# Needlyy brand direction

The recommended logo is a linked **N**: two rounded strokes represent a customer and a professional connecting. It is compact enough for app navigation and favicons, while the wordmark keeps the full name readable.

- Primary symbol: `public/brand/needlyy-mark.svg` (scalable, no raster background).
- Green `#15803d`: action, local growth, and the customer connection.
- Blue `#2563eb`: professional skills and the seller connection.
- Navy: existing navigation and dashboard anchor colour.
- Use the mark beside “Needlyy” in headers. Use the mark alone only where space is limited.
- Preserve its square proportions and clear space. Do not add shadows or gradients to the mark.

The SVG uses a white rounded holding shape so its two colours remain clear on navy navigation. Hero artwork is supplied separately for desktop and mobile; it depicts illustrative characters rather than actual providers. The supporting toolkit PNG has a transparent background. Interface icons remain consistent Lucide line icons for crisp rendering at every screen size.
